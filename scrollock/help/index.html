Getting started

Why does Scrollock need Screen Time access?

Blocking on iPhone works only through Apple's Screen Time. Scrollock never sees which apps you use — iOS gives it anonymous tokens and does the blocking itself.

What session types are there?

Block Now blocks apps right away for a set time. Schedule blocks them during set hours on chosen days. Time Limit blocks them after you've used them for a set time each day.

Blocking

An app isn't being blocked

  • Check that Screen Time access is still on: iOS Settings › Screen Time › Apps With Screen Time Access › Scrollock.
  • Make sure the session is enabled and active on the Home tab.
  • If you just changed a session, close the blocked app completely and open it again.

How do I take a break?

Tap Pause blocking on the Home tab. Free users can pause for 10 minutes; Pro users can choose the length.

What is Hard Mode?

Hard Mode (Pro) stops you from pausing, editing or deleting a session while it is blocking. Use it when you really need to stay focused — there is no way to cancel it early from the app.

How do I delete a session?

Open the session from Home and tap Delete. You'll be asked to type a short commitment sentence first, so you don't delete it on impulse.

Scrollock Pro

What's in Pro?

Unlimited sessions of every type, Hard Mode, weekly and monthly reports, custom pause length, a custom shield message, unlimited Unbrainrot games and no ads.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Open iOS Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Scrollock and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to avoid being charged. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

I bought Pro but it's not unlocked

Go to Settings in Scrollock and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Does Scrollock see my apps or browsing?

No. Your Screen Time data stays on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How do I stop personalized ads?

Choose "Ask App Not to Track" when iOS asks, or turn tracking off in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Pro users see no ads.
