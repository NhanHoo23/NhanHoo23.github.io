Scrollock

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

Scrollock ("the app", "we") helps you block distracting apps and websites with Apple's Screen Time. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Scrollock is developed by Ho Huu Trung, an individual developer based in Vietnam.

The short version
  • Your Screen Time data — which apps you use, which apps you block and for how long — never leaves your device.
  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and there is no account.
  • We collect anonymous usage events to improve the app, identified only by a random ID.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Screen Time data stays on your device

Scrollock uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity). The apps, categories and websites you pick are given to the app as opaque tokens by iOS — Scrollock cannot see their names. Usage reports such as screen time, pickups and notifications are drawn by an iOS extension on your device and are not readable by the app or sent anywhere. Your blocking sessions are stored only on your device.

2. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand how the app is used and fix problems, Scrollock sends usage events to our analytics database hosted by Supabase. Each event contains:

A random ID created on first launchA random string, not linked to your Apple ID or device identifiers
Event name and details"session created", session type, duration, number of apps selected (a count, never app names), paywall shown, purchase result
App stateApp version, iOS version, free or Pro, plan type, number of active sessions, game best scores, install week
TimeWhen the event happened

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising identifier for analytics. We use this data only to improve Scrollock, and we never sell it.

3. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows an ad when you open the app and lets you watch an optional rewarded ad for an extra game in Unbrainrot. Pro users see no ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device information, your IP address and, only if you allow it, your advertising identifier (IDFA) to show and measure ads.

  • App Tracking Transparency: iOS asks whether Scrollock may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your IDFA. You can change this anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform lets you accept, reject or manage how your data is used for ads. You can change your choice later.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Purchases

Scrollock Pro subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive only whether a purchase is active, the product and its price — never your name, Apple ID or payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

5. Notifications and permissions

Notifications (for example, a reminder two days before a free trial ends) are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notifications. The app asks for Screen Time and notification permission only to provide its features; you can turn them off in iOS Settings.

6. Data retention and deletion

Analytics events are kept for as long as they are useful to improve the app, and no longer than 24 months. Because analytics are tied only to a random ID, we cannot identify you from them. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device, including that ID, so events sent after a reinstall are not linked to earlier ones. For any deletion request, email us and we will help.

7. Your rights (GDPR and CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our legal bases for processing are your consent (personalized ads) and our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it (anonymous analytics).

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for money. Information used by AdMob for personalized ads may be considered "sharing" under the CCPA; you can opt out by choosing "Ask App Not to Track" or by rejecting consent in the ad consent form. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Scrollock does not honor "Do Not Track" browser signals because it is an app, not a website, but you can control tracking with the iOS options above.

8. Children

Scrollock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Service providers and data transfers

We use Supabase (analytics hosting), Google AdMob (ads) and Apple (purchases, Screen Time). These providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under their own privacy safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
